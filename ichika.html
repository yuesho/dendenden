Ichika Nakano

Ichika Nakano (中野 一花) is the first of the quintuplets in the manga and anime series Gotoubun no Hanayome, or The Quintessential Quintuplets. She is the eldest of the five sisters and serves as one of the main love interests for Futaro Uesugi. Ichika is depicted as an aspiring actress who balances her studies with her acting career. She is a responsible and caring older sister, and often takes on a motherly role among the sisters. In terms of her personality, she is friendly, outgoing, and has a positive attitude, making her popular among her classmates. However, she can also be insecure and sensitive, particularly about her own shortcomings and her relationship with her sisters. Throughout the series, Ichika faces various challengers in her career and love life, including conflicts with her sisters over Futaro's attention and a difficult decision about whether to pursue her acting career or focus on her studies. Overall, Ichika's character is multi-faceted, with her ambition and emotional depth making her a compelling and relatable figure in the series.

Her Appearance

Ichika is a girl with very short hair and has a single long strand on the right side. She has white-pink hair color, ranging from oyster pink to a light pink shade in Volume 1, and oyster white to light pink shade in Volume 4. Her notable accessory is a single earring. Like her other sisters, she has dark blue eyes, an average height, as well as a well-endowed figure. Ichika wears her school uniform with a thin jacket tied to her waist, sometimes with an unbuttoned top; revealing her cleavage. She is also shown to wear a handbag.

Her Personality

Ichika acts as the eldest sister of the quintuplets, as she seems to have a calm and composed demeanor; making herself an approachable person. Futaro noted that her smile is fake and encouraged her to put away that kind of smile. She initially had a pessimistic attitude towards her sisters' learning capabilities, making her one of the obstacles for Futaro's tutoring lessons early in the story. After being inspired, she began to change her mind and started taking studying seriously. He also helped her fix any issues she had with her sisters; making them closer than ever before. Despite her smug expression and teasing behavior, Ichika is shown to have a weakness to romantic moments happening to her; blushing whenever thoughts about it come to her mind. According to Futaro, each of the quintuplets have their own characteristics. On Ichika's case, her writing and paper would be sloppy. She also remarked to pick things up quickly and is troubled when her sisters make decisions without her presence. As her pride of being the eldest sister, she plans to be successful first before telling her sisters about the job. She is shown to study in between her filming; whether be it inside a car on the road or late at night. Futaro was also impressed on how she was able to balance her work and school.
