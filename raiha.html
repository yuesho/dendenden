Some Quick Facts

  • Full Name: Raiha Uesugi
  • Date of Birth: February 14th, 2005
  • What is her wish? Her wish is for her brother, Futaro Uesugi, to break out of his isolated, study-obsessed life, make friends, find happiness, and not waste his youth.
  • Voice Actor: Natsumi Takamori

Raiha Uesugi

Raiha Uesugi (上杉 らいは) is the younger sister of Futaro Uesugi. She is the one who cooks for the family, and depsite still being in elementary school, she's wise beyond her year.

Her Appearance

Raiha is a slim young girl who stands at a medium height with long black hair, green eyes in season 1, and brown in season 2. She has a simple butterfly-shaped ribbon, holding up her hair in a high cowlick.

His Personality

Raiha is a cheerful girl who is essentially the head of the family, despite her young age, since she's the one who takes care of the financial matters, leaving her to hold back a lot of things a child normally do. she cares a lot about her brother and concerned about his life, even when both of them have grown up significantly.
