Nino Nakano

Nino Nakano (中野二乃) is one of the main characters in the manga and anime series Gotoubun no Hanayome. She is the second of the quintuplets, and serves as one of the main love interests for Futaro. Nino is depicted as a confident, strong-willed and assertive character who is fiercely protective of her sisters. She is also known for her culinary skills, which she uses to care for her family. She is often depicted as being blunt and direct, with a no-nonsense attitude towards life. However, she can also be emotional and vulnerable, particularly when it comes to her relationships with her sisters and Futaro. Throughout the series, Nino's character undergoes a significant development as she struggles to come to terms with her feelings for Futaro and conflicts with her sisters over his attention. Nino's character arc is intertwined with the series' themes of love, family, and self-discovery, and her interactions with other characters provide some of the series' most emotional and impactful moments. Overall, her character is dynamic and multi-dimensional with her fierce determination and emotional complexity making her a compelling figure in the series.

Her Appearance

Nino is a cute, but cold-looking girl with waist-length straight hair, later cut to shoulder-length in Volume 4, Chapter 46 and shown in Volume 5, Chapter 47, that has square bangs hanging over her eyebrows. She has reddish-pink hair, ranging from light pink to telemagenta in Volume 1, and antique pink to red-violet in Volume 4. Her accessories are twin butterfly-shaped ribbons on both sides of her head. Like her sisters, she has dark blue eyes, an average height, and a well-endowed figure. She wears a long-sleeved sweater that covers her uniform and a pair of white thigh-high socks. Just like the eldest sister, Nino uses a handbag. She is also noted to be the more fashionable one of the quintuplets, as she has various cute accessories and takes care of her nails. Her ears are yet to be pierced, but she plans to have them done at least before marriage.

Her Personality

Nino has an outgoing personality and is socially aware, resulting in her having many friends and is a social butterfly. She has a sharp tongue and talks bluntly when confronting others, even to her sisters. Her rebellious attitude makes her the least cooperative girl among the sisters, making her archetype resemble that of a "Tsundere". Despite that cold and rude front, she is caring and protective, like when she was touched by Futaro's past; remarking that it is tearjerking. It is also evident by her willingness to act as the cook of the family, ignoring her care towards her own appearance during culinary activities. She is also shown to always have band-aids with her, possibly as precaution to Yotsuba Nakano's behavior.

Her interests seem to lie more on romance than anything else. An example is when she wanted to watch a romance movie whilst Itsuki Nakano preferred a science documentary instead. With this in mind, she likes to openly state her feelings for Futaro, regardless whether it's in front of him or her sisters. She will also file her papers neatly.
