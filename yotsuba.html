Yotsuba Nakano

Yotsuba Nakano (中野四葉) is one of the main characters in the manga and anime series Gotoubun no Hanayome. She is the fourth of the quintuplets, and serves as one of the main love interests for Futaro. She is initially depicted as a cheerful and outgoing character who is always willing to lend a hand to others. She is known for her energetic personality and her love for sports, particularly track and field. Yotsuba's character is distinguished by her trademark green ribbon, which she wears on her head. She becomes a representative for Class 3-1 later in the story. Throughout the series, her character undergoes significant development as she grapples with her own internal conflicts and emotional traumas. Her character's arc is intertwined with the series' theme of self-discovery, acceptance and personal growth. Her interactions with other characters, particularly Futaro, provide some of the series' most poignant and heartwarming moments. Her character's journey is particularly compelling as she strives to overcome her own personal struggles and find a sense of happiness and fulfillment in her relationships with others.

Her Appearance

Yotsuba is a fair-skinned teenage girl with short, straight hair that stops just around her neck. While her actual hair color is pink, she is portrayed to have a light-orange hair color. Her most notable accessory is a bunny ear-shaped ribbon. She wears her uniform with a large butterfly-shaped tie tie and wears a sports bag.

Her Personality

Yotsuba is an active, cheerful, and easygoing girl. She talks in a loud, spontaneous and sometimes absent-minded manner. She is the type of person who can't say no to people who need help, and is mostly seen helping others; either directly or indirectly. While cheerful on the outside towards others, she she harbours feelings of inferiority towards her sisters and her own abilities. Because of this, she grew a desire to be different from her sisters. She shortened her hair, put on a ribbon, and excelled in extra-curricular activites.
