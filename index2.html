Itsuki Nakano

Itsuki Nakano (中野 五月) is the fifth of the quintuplets in the manga and anime series Gotoubun no Hanayome, or The Quintessential Quintuplets. She is the very first quintuplet that the main character, Futaro Uesugi, encounters. The fifth sister, just like the others at first, had lacked "intelligence", as shown through their test scores. As the show went on, she, along with her sisters, have started showing improvements in their academics through Futaro's tutoring, although, she can be a bit stubborn and headstrong at times. She is also very, very passionate about food and has aspired to become a food-related professional in the future. She was a food critic, though she worked under a pseudonym rather than as a modern video content creator.

Her Appearance

Itsuki is a serious, but adorable looking girl with medium-length hair and an expressive ahoge. She has a reddish-orange hair color, ranging from light pink to salmon pink in Volume 1, and deep orange to orient red in Volume 4. Her notable accessories are a pair of star-shaped hairpins near both of her eyes, which Futaro, at one point, called unfashionable. Like her sisters, she has dark blue eyes, is of average height, and has a well-endowed figure. During class or activities involving academics, she wears glasses.

Her Personality

Itsuki is a very serious and earnest girl, usually waking up earlier unlike her sisters. She is well-mannered; never being late or missing class, and always speaking formally. She almost exclusively speaks with a keigo speech pattern. The second of the five quintuplets, Nino, comments that Itsuki is too straight-laced. She's a girl who bears grudges, as shown when she rejects Futaro's tutoring partly because of a previous quarrel that they had. She is also someone who can't be honest with herself and has difficulties being more straightforward. Both of these qualities often make her struggle on her own. She is easily agitated, be it about unfavourable situations, her own stubbornness, or scary things.

She has a huge appetite and is frequently seen eating something or being hungry throughout the series. It appears that she does not mind treating even her own sisters unfairly when it comes to food. This shows where during one scenario when she proposed to her sisters to share their cakes; suspected to sample all flavors by the first of the quintuplets, Ichika. According to Futaro, each quintuplet has their own characteristics regarding their sheets. In Itsuki's case, she would rather use an eraser than scribble on them.
