Futaro Uesugi

Futaro Uesugi (上杉風太郎) is the main character in the manga and anime series Gotoubun no Hanayome. He is a student at Asahiyama High School who lives with his father, Isanari Uesugi, and his younger sister Raiha Uesugi, in a small apartment. As a hardworking and highly intelligent student, Futaro excels and is at the top of his class. Due to the financial difficulties faced by his family, he takes on the opportunity to serve as the tutor for the Nakano quintuplets. Eventually, he would end up marrying one of them, whose identity was one of the biggest mysteries of the series until it was revealed near the end of the series. Without a mother, his sister took on the household responsibilities, including cooking. As a result of eating simple and inexpensive meals his entire life, he lacks the ability to distinguish between good an poorly cooked food. At the time of his wedding, his financial situation has not significantly improved.

Five years prior to the beginning of the story, Futaro went on a school trip to Kyoto and met a girl his age who inspired him to work hard and become a responsible adult who can support his family, especially his sister. He unfortunately did not ask for the girl's name. He then attends Asahiyama High School, where he is known for his exceptional academic performance, regularly earning the perfect grades. Despite his amazing academic record, he has limited social interactions and only has his father and his sister in his phone's contacts.

His Appearance

Futaro is a young man with fair skin and black hair styled in a bowl cut with two strands sticking up from the back of his head. Nino comments on his hairstyle, calling it weird, which he claims was cut by his sister Raiha. He has bangs that cover his forehead and his eye color varied across the anime's adaptation (blue in the first season, yellow during the second season and in the manga). He has a slim physique and a tall build, although he tends to slouch at times. He is noted to be quite handsome. He is often portrayed with a serious expression, giving off a scary and thuggish vibe, to which Miku referred to as the "face of a bad guy". Despite this, Nino develops a crush on him, and on a younger Futaro who had a delinquent-like appearance, despite initially dismissing his looks as being dull-looking.

He typically wears the standard uniform of Asahiyama High School, both inside and outside of the campus. The uniform includes a short-sleeved white shirt, dark blue trousers, and a white belt. During cooler weathers, he wears a light-blue V-neck sweater, which is also a part of his uniform, over over his shirt. For footwear, he typically wears black sneakers with white soles and laces for everyday wear. After the Labor Thanksgiving Arc, however, it has been revealed that he also wears high-top sneakers with similar coloring. During the School Field Trip Arc, he wore the clothes Nino picked out for him. This includes a khaki parka jacket over a light-color mock turtleneck long-sleeved shirt, which appears black in the anime. It is not shown whether he wore it again after the field trip. During his Flashback Arc, a hospitalized Futaro is seen wearing a set of plain pajamas, which he seems to use as nightwear. He wore it again during the Seven Goodbyes Arc when he goes out for a night walk with Itsuki.

In his childhood, Futaro sports a blonde, spikey slicked-back hair style, much similar to the wig he wore when disguising as Kintaro. When Nino first met Kintaro, she immediately notices the latter was the boy in the picture. He also wore an earring in his left ear, which he removed when he got older.

His Personality

According to his father, Futaro used to be similar to him as a kid with their wild personality, being bad at studying, lives a frugal lifestyle, and prefers solitude. Ichika remarks that Futaro doesn't mince his words. Yotsuba's first impression of him is gloomy and the type of guy to not have many friends. His facial expression is always serious, sometimes having a grumpy and annoyed look, and rarely gets flustered. As he grows older, he becomes more of a study freak, growing more pompous and asocial. Despite his initial facade, he is actually a very caring person, especially towards his sister, Raiha. He is noted to have a big ego. He is also straightforward, tactless, brutally honest, and rude; but is actually capable of holding back his emotions. Despite that, he could momentarily lose his composure when agitated and faced with unfavorable circumstances. He initially did not like getting involved with other people's problems, finding them to be troublesome. Despite his impertinence, he is knowledgeable enough in knowing what to say in specific situations when thrown at him; rarely giving up when trying to achieve a specific objective.

Even with his egotistical nature, Futaro is a very hardworking individual, with high amounts of patience; especially when getting the attention of the quintuplets and tutoring them despite the amounts of failures and setbacks. He later shows a sense of duty, strong enough to help the quintuplets in their studies despite them not paying him back immediately, even though the money that came with the tutoring was the initial reason he accepted the job. He also begins valuing their relationship, to the point where he would do things outside of their tutor-student connection, which would later on bloom into a point where they mutually support each other with little question. Despite being bad at sports and lacking a fair amount of stamina, he is rarely shown to be tired for the most part; able to work part-time jobs beside tutoring; one being a helper in a pastry shop.
