Miku Nakano

Miku Nakano (中野三玖) is one of the main characters in the manga and anime series Gotoubun no Hanayome. She is the third of the quintuplets, and serves as one of the main love interests for Futaro. She's initially depicted as a shy and introverted character who struggles to express herself and communicate with others. However, as the series progresses, she begins to come out of her shell and develop her own sense of confidence and individuality. Miku is particularly interested in literature and her love for books is a central part of her character's identity. Throughout the series, her character undergoes crucial development as she navigates her feelings for Futaro and conflicts with her sisters over his attention. Her interactions with other characters provide some of the series' most emotional moments, particularly as she grows closer to Futaro and begins to assert herself more confidently with her family and social circles.

Her Appearance

Miku is an emotionless looking girl with medium-length hair, with strands hanging over the right side of her face. She wears a set of blue wireless headphones that have a triangle-shaped logo on the earcups (Audio-Technica). She usually wears a long-sleeved sweater that covers her uniform, along with black tights. She also wears a ranzel.

Her Personality

Miku is a silent and reserved girl who has a pessimistic way of thinking and low confidence, even to the point of belittling herself. In contrary to her reserved self, she can talk in a straightforward manner and doesn't hesitate to confront others. She can also be slightly possessive, as she doesn't react well when Yotsuba kisses Futaro on the cheek to wipe some cream and overall she gets pouty whenever another girl, especially one of her sisters, gets a little to close for comfort with Futaro. It is shown that she has an obsession with generals from the Sengoku Period. She is greatly influenced by their philosophies, to the point of affecting her real-life behavior. She hid this fact from everyone and got embarrassed when Futaro found out. When he challenged her to a duel of historical knowledge, Miku donned a set of armor similar to Takeda Shingen's.

She has a depressed sense of modesty and cares little about her appearance compared to her sisters; not even acknowledging situations where her body is exposed. She didn't mind removing her long socks in front of her tutor or bother wearing flashy clothing. Despite this, She doesn't seem to harbor insecurities about her own appearance, as she confides to Nino that she doesn't view herself as cute compared to her and Ichika. She also has good handwriting.
